I'm a data scientist with 13 years of experience building pipelines, models, and tools on complex real-world data. I specialize in time-series analysis, machine learning, experimental design, and translating quantitative results into decisions. I'm currently looking for data science and analytics roles in industry.

My portfolio includes a [production ML pipeline](2026/01/11/spikejungle) for cleaning large-scale neural datasets, an [original data science curriculum](2026/01/08/lab-in-justice-data-science) with peer-reviewed student outcomes, and [scientific visualizations](2026/01/10/scientific-visualizations) spanning behavior, computation, and neuroscience.

<img width=400 src='/assets/images/je_headshot.jpg'>

I completed my PhD at Columbia University (Psychology 2019-2025) in [Sarah Woolley's lab](https://woolleylab.com/). My [dissertation research](https://academiccommons.columbia.edu/doi/10.7916/7bt6-xb79) was funded by an [NIH predoctoral grant (F31)](https://reporter.nih.gov/search/ipd8KKjb7kqwONlKDV9PNw/project-details/10607688) and was a study of constraints on the temporal and sequential organization of birdsong, a biological system where controlled experiments support causal inference about vocal learning, with direct implications for understanding speech. Alongside my research, I created and taught an original, highly-rated [course in applied data science](2026/01/08/lab-in-justice-data-science) for advanced Columbia students across disciplines, many of whom had no prior coding experience and direct personal stakes in the policy questions we examined.

Columbia's Zuckerman Institute [told my story](https://zuckermaninstitute.columbia.edu/science-life-jacob-edwards) in pictures.
